## Abstract

This research investigates whether the Algorapolis architecture would prevent, detect, or correct the civilizational failure mode now converging from opposite ideological directions: the erosion of a polity's capacity to physically produce what its survival requires. The trigger is a live international discourse — the American reindustrialization debate crystallized in Senator Marco Rubio's post-Cold War autopsy — whose diagnosis is substantially correct: a generation of cost-optimized sourcing under the premise that *where things are made does not matter* produced measurable adversary dependency (munitions runway ratio ≈ 0.01; 90% adversary rare-earth refining; 232x shipyard asymmetry) while remaining invisible inside GDP. This research demonstrates that the proposed cure is itself a second failure mode: energy-dominance leverage (the US–Venezuela NABEP arrangement, verified here from the White House fact sheet of August 31, 2026) and unguarded reindustrialization replicate in the cure the pathology the diagnosis identified in the disease. Drawing on seven natural experiments (the WWII arsenal mobilization, Japan's MITI developmental state, Germany's Russia-gas dependency and its rupture, Tanzania's Ujamaa villagization and Latin American import substitution, China's industrial overmatch and its brittleness ledger, the US deindustrialization itself, and the CHIPS Act as live test), on the convergent statutory precedent (EU Critical Raw Materials Act, Japan's Economic Security Promotion Act, India's PLI, the IEA reserve system), and on the sanctions-adaptation literature, this research specifies the doctrine of **Strategic Sufficiency without Adversarial Dominance**: twelve new Design Principles (DP-PC-1 through DP-PC-12) covering criticality machinery, adversary-graded thresholds, sourcing Modality-Matching, arsenal continuity, the skills substrate, mineral and processing depth, energy anti-dominance, protectionist capture guards, multi-administration continuity, just-in-case reserves, and blockade graceful degradation. The research also registers Study 16's orphaned DP-ESG-1..7 series (drafted, never wired into the registry, architecture, or simulation) upgraded with anti-capture circuitry, and specifies Civilization Simulation V6 (industrial_capacity metric, adversary_dependency state, three trade regimes, five new shocks) which makes both failure modes runnable and the doctrine falsifiable.

**Keywords:** industrial sovereignty, strategic sufficiency, deindustrialization, supply chain weaponization, economic security, munitions runway, critical minerals, energy dominance, autarky, Ujamaa, developmental state, anti-capture, reindustrialization

## Purpose and Scope

The Algorapolis framework is ahead of existing governance systems on the adjacent domains: its digital-sovereignty doctrine (Priority Gap Part I) already treats cables, chips, and cloud concentration as constitutional questions; its Software Supply Chain Integrity Module (SLSA L3+, DP-7/CYB-7) already specifies provenance and attestation for the software layer; its offline-resilience and MVA (Manual Verification Authority) architecture already assumes infrastructure loss; its long-termism is native rather than imported (Volume I/II, the Ark, high-continuity assets); and its anti-capture machinery (Study 18's Civilizational Immune System) is first-class. The education research established learning as a substrate (EDU-13, EDU-16, EDU-17) with a Proof-of-Skill layer.

What no layer, module, or principle specifies is the physical production function itself. The cross-check conducted for this research (Part VIII gap analysis) confirms five structural absences. First, there is no doctrine for domestic production capacity of critical physical goods — semiconductors, munitions, shipbuilding, pharmaceuticals, energy, processed minerals — anywhere between Layer 4 (Economics), Layer 5 (Technology), and Layer 6 (Security). Second, the Modality-Matching Principle — the framework's foundational rule that governance tools must match domain risk modalities — has never been applied to sourcing decisions, the single largest class of production-shaping choices a polity makes. Third, the education-to-production pipeline exists on neither side: the education principles do not link to capacity planning, and no capacity principle links to occupational pipelines. Fourth, energy and minerals appear in the framework only as monitoring objects (Economic Telemetry, Infrastructure Awareness), never as strategic-capability doctrine. Fifth — the finding that supplied this research's integration core — Study 16 (*Geopolitical Supply Chains and Economic Resilience*) already drafted a complete DP-ESG-1..7 series covering supply-chain resilience, chokepoint diversification, developmental-state capabilities, mineral geopolitics preparedness, monetary multipolarity, adaptive trade architecture, and geopolitical positioning, complete with TSMC/ASML concentration analysis, CHIPS Act assessment, JIT-to-JIC reasoning, and Russia sanctions-evasion modeling; the series is orphaned, absent from the 177-principle registry, absent from the architecture, absent from the schema, and unreflected in the simulation.

The gap analysis also confirms the simulation blind spot: Civilization Simulation V4's seventeen shock types contain no trade embargo, no sanctions regime, and no supply-chain weaponization; its war shock models damage, not adversary-dependency amplification; and no metric measures industrial capacity. A framework that cannot run the efficiency trap as a scenario cannot claim to have stress-tested against it.

A note on analytical posture: this research follows the framework's threat-agnostic method (Study 18). The object of analysis is the structural mechanism — two failure modes that arrive from opposite ideological directions — never the identity, sincerity, or partisanship of any movement or statesman. The trigger transcript is treated as a claims ledger to be verified, corrected, and where necessary refuted: of its load-bearing claims, roughly half verified, three refuted or garbled, one unverifiable, and the host's demographic-replacement framing is excluded from the evidence base as ideology rather than analysis. Honesty about contested causal claims — including the debt-to-radicalization thesis this research declines to encode as causation — is what distinguishes a governance science from an ideological tract. The framework's Ujamaa heritage matters here in both directions: it grounds a self-reliance doctrine in African experience, and it supplies that doctrine's own failure mode (villagization, parastatal soft budgets) as the internal counter-balance against the autarky trap. A doctrine authored from Tanzania must be the doctrine Tanzania's own history stress-tests.

## SECTION 1: THE PROBLEM — TWO TRAPS, NOT ONE

### 1.1 What the Existing Locks Protect

The framework's current protections are real but partial. Digital sovereignty governs information infrastructure; software supply-chain integrity governs code provenance; offline resilience governs operation under infrastructure loss; long-termism governs time horizons; anti-capture governs institutional corruption. None of these governs the production function. A polity could satisfy every one of them and still discover — as the United States did in January 2023, in a think-tank wargame rather than any procurement dashboard — that its critical munitions exhaust in under one week against 18–24-month replacement lead times. The locks protect the nervous system; the trap seizes the body.

### 1.2 Failure Mode 1: The Globalist Efficiency Trap

The mechanism, in five empirically anchored steps. **Step one — cost-optimization as sole sourcing criterion.** Post-Cold War doctrine assumed efficiency and resilience were compatible, or that resilience was purchasable on demand; the wargame record and the COVID-19 just-in-time collapse disproved the second half, and the dependency data the first. **Step two — capacity atrophy is invisible in GDP.** US output rose while manufacturing employment fell from 19.6 million to roughly 12.6 million and approximately 91,000 plants closed: the trap operates inside a growing economy, which is why conventional macroeconomic monitoring does not catch it. **Step three — dependency concentrates at chokepoints.** China refines ~90% of rare earths; Taiwan fabricates the majority of advanced logic; 13–17% direct API exposure becomes ~47% measured on the production function; the ONI slide documents 232x shipyard asymmetry. Concentration, not trade volume, is the threat variable. **Step four — runway collapse is discovered only at war-gaming.** The CSIS *First Battle of the Next War* series found LRASM-class munitions exhaust in under one week at 450–1,000 missiles per week of consumption; the normalized runway ratio (depletion days ÷ replenishment days) is ≈ 0.01. A polity that measures capability counts but not burn-rate runway is structurally blind to this trap. **Step five — adjustment failure compounds politically.** The China Shock literature's core finding is not "trade is bad" but "adjustment failed": affected local labor markets never recovered, and the follow-on work (Autor et al. 2020) documents the polarization channel — the efficiency trap degrades the industrial base and the democratic substrate simultaneously.

### 1.3 Failure Mode 2: The Autarky/Leverage Trap

The cure can be worse than the disease in two variants. **Variant A — rent-seeking capture, the domestic face.** The empirical signature is identical across Tanzania's Ujamaa villagization (11 million people moved; food exporter to food importer), Latin American import-substitution industrialization (100%+ effective protection rates, parastatal soft budgets), Japan's zombie-firm equilibrium (convoy finance and evergreen lending capturing the system for fifteen years), the US sugar program ($2.4–4 billion per year to roughly 10,000 growers), Jones Act lock-in, and F-35 incentive-fee capture: *support without expiry, plus no external examiner, plus no exit path*. Security framing is the modern legal accelerant — WTO Article XXI's self-judging character enabled 2018 steel protection on "national security" grounds, and the World Bank documents systematic sunset-clause erosion. **Variant B — coercive leverage, the external face.** The specimen is live: the US–Venezuela NABEP arrangement (blockade December 2025; the January 3, 2026 raid that captured Maduro; the August 31, 2026 White House fact sheet "…Historic Oil Agreement to Secure American Energy Dominance…") — 100-year concessions over 17 fields, ~65 billion barrels of claimed (unaudited) reserves, a 35% zero-cost equity stake held by the Pentagon, 20% of output purchasable at production cost, right of first refusal on the remaining 80%, board-veto power with a US-citizen majority, up to $100 billion of unfunded investment promises, and ~$200 billion in royalties over 25 years. The same variant is fielded by the adversary: China's 2023–2025 gallium, germanium, antimony, and rare-earth export controls, including the November 2025 timed suspension deployed as explicit negotiating leverage. The empirical law across every precedent — the 1973 embargo (which created the IEA and the strategic reserves), Russia's gas weaponization (EU share 45% to 13%), China's mineral controls (which financed MP Materials, forced the EU CRMA, and boomeranged into China's own suspension) — is that **weaponization destroys the weapon on a 1–10 year clock**, but the coerced polity pays a real transition price (Germany's energy-intensive output: −15.2%). Dominance plays have negative net present value beyond roughly five to ten years, and a governance architecture that treats chokepoints as standing assets is modeling a depreciating — indeed self-liquidating — liability as capital.

### 1.4 The Two-Front Threat Model

The two modes are not alternatives; they are a pincer, and the historical record shows each mode curing into the other. The Venezuela play is reindustrialization doctrine *plus* Failure Mode 2 Variant B — the cure carrying the disease. Conversely, a pure-market globalism with no sufficiency floors is Failure Mode 1 operating as the cure for perceived autarky. A doctrine that blocks only one mode is a capture instrument for the other; this is the same symmetric structure the Family and Social Fabric research (Part VII) established for its two-front threat model, and it requires the same architecture: locks that bind regardless of which ideology is ascendent. The economic asymmetry that makes a two-front defense feasible: **sufficiency floors are cheap and self-limiting** (IMF-calibrated: friend-shoring ~1.8% of global GDP against reshoring ~4.5%; stockpiles cheaper still), while **dominance positions are expensive and self-liquidating** (China's overmatch coexists with 290% debt/GDP and $8–12 trillion of hidden local-government debt). A doctrine bounded by cost ceilings is affordable by construction; a doctrine bounded by leverage ambitions is not.

### 1.5 SLE Design Implication

The SLE must treat productive capacity as a constitutional metric domain, exactly as it treats privacy and family integrity: monitored as stocks (not flows), alarmed at thresholds (not reviewed annually), and locked against both capture directions. The design requires a criticality machinery (what counts as critical, scored and sunsettable), adversary-graded thresholds (how much is enough, and how much adversary concentration is too much), a coded intervention conjunction (when market sourcing may be overridden, and through which cheapest-first ladder), anti-capture circuitry on every override instrument, and a no-dominance mirror cap on the polity's own external positions. Each component exists in embryonic form somewhere in world statute; nowhere do all five exist in one system, and nowhere does the missing component — anti-capture circuitry — exist at all. That asymmetry is Algorapolis's design opportunity.

## SECTION 2: THE TRIGGER DISCOURSE AND THE CLAIMS LEDGER

### 2.1 The Diagnosis Under Examination

The trigger transcript's core argument, stated fairly: post-Cold War elites assumed exporting capitalism would liberalize China and that interdependence would pacify great-power relations; the assumption failed because the Chinese state kept its population's consumption suppressed (~38–40% of GDP against 68% in the US) and its industrial policy intact; the United States, as reserve-currency issuer, traded paper for goods and deindustrialized; the resulting adversary dependency is a national-security catastrophe measured in munitions runways, shipbuilding asymmetry, and mineral chokepoints; the remedy is reindustrialization — minerals and processing, factories, and skilled technicians — sustained across multiple administrations, with education redefined around employability. This is a coherent civilizational diagnosis, and its load-bearing empirical claims are examined below. The add-ons supplied by the hosting commentary — war-game specifics, shipbuilding multiples, the Venezuela energy-leverage play, and a debt-to-radicalization thesis — are treated as separate claims requiring separate verification, because conflating diagnosis with amplification is how ideological systems import errors.

### 2.3 The Provenance Firewall

The cruise-missile claim's genealogy — Chinese state-television footage of missile *motors*, relayed by a columnist as "allegedly," amplified through commentary into a weekly-versus-annual production ratio quoted by politicians — is a metric-corruption case study in miniature, and it generalizes. The SLE's quantitative inputs must carry provenance classes: independent measurement, official claim, state-media claim, expert estimate, viral amplification. Policy thresholds may cite classes one and four only; state-media and viral-amplified figures may enter the evidentiary record solely as objects of provenance analysis, never as calibrations. This is the sensor layer of the doctrine: an architecture that cannot distinguish a sourced 232x from a viral 350x will eventually legislate the 350x.

## SECTION 3: THE NATURAL EXPERIMENTS

The empirical record of what happens when polities implement either failure mode — or the doctrine between them — supplies seven cases covering the parameter space: market-driven versus state-driven, voluntary versus coerced, wartime versus peacetime, and both traps in both variants.

### 3.1 The WWII Arsenal of Democracy (1941–1945) — The Positive Specification

The United States produced roughly two-thirds of Allied materiel, pushing output from ~2% to ~40% of GNP: 2,710 Liberty ships (median build time 244 days falling to 39, with a record of 4 days 15.5 hours), a B-24 bomber every ~63 minutes at Willow Run, under War Production Board priority authority, Defense Plant Corporation risk-socialized finance (government-owned plants, private operation), time-boxed emergency powers, and excess-profits renegotiation. The transferable asset was not output volume but *fungibility*: a deep stock of tacit-knowledge carriers (machinists, welders, production engineers), conversion governance enabling month-scale switching, and risk-bearing public finance. The 40%-of-GNP conversion ceiling is the calibration datum for what "sufficient" means; the honest claim-audit (the "19 million workers" figure mischaracterizes female labor-force data; the ice-cream-barge anecdote is apocryphal in attribution) is part of the case's evidentiary discipline. Classification: **positive specification** — conversion architecture, not stockpile volume, and not standing autarky, is the mechanism; the case grounds DP-PC-5, DP-PC-6, DP-PC-9, and DP-PC-10, and is specified as Case Study 12.

### 3.2 Japan's MITI Developmental State (1950s–1990s) — The Dual Specification

The VLSI research consortium (1976–1980) contributed to Japan reaching ~50% of world semiconductor share; industrial targeting under export discipline produced globally competitive autos, electronics, and precision machinery. The dual record: Beason and Weinstein's econometrics find support flowing disproportionately to *declining* sectors rather than promoted winners; convoy finance and zombie lending (Caballero, Hoshi, and Kashyap 2008) kept insolvent borrowers alive for fifteen years, suppressing productivity growth in the 1990s; and MITI's own wrong bets (resisting autos, the fifth-generation computing flop) show the planner's information problem. Classification: **dual specification** — mission consortia with legal lifespans and export-benchmarked performance tests are incorporable (the Amsden reciprocity standard: subsidies conditional on performance, withdrawal on failure); non-terminable support, convoy finance, and examiner-free sectoral planning are the negative. The case supplies DP-PC-9's empirical core and the registered DP-ESG-3's upgrade.

### 3.3 Germany's Russia-Gas Dependency (1990s–2022) — The Coercibility Datum

Germany's 55% gas dependency on Russia (2021) converted, on February 2022 and after, into the coercible variable the doctrine predicts: an energy shock that permanently destroyed 15.2% of energy-intensive industrial output (February 2022–March 2026), produced Gazprom's first loss in more than twenty years, and opened a persistent price gap (gas ~4.5x, electricity ~2.6x versus US levels). The polity's adaptive capacity was real — floating LNG terminals contracted and built in roughly nine months, rationing avoided, direct Russian pipeline gas to ~0 by 2023 — and "Wandel durch Handel" (change through trade) died as doctrine: the exact mirror image of the American "capitalism exports democracy" premise, failing for the same reason (regimes do not trade themselves out of control). Classification: **dual specification** — the 55% threshold is the coercibility datum (dependency measured on the adversary-adjusted production function is the DP-PC-3 variable); the weaponization-destroys-the-weapon dynamic and the nine-month FSRU substitution bound the leverage half-life; the −15.2% output loss prices the coerced side's transition.

### 3.4 Ujamaa and ISI (1967–1985; 1950–1980s) — The Author's Heritage as Internal Stress-Test

Tanzania's Arusha Declaration (1967) declared self-reliance (ujamaa na kujitegemea) and villagization moved more than 11 million people into ~7,500+ villages; parastatal expansion, soft budgets, and forced agricultural reorganization converted a food exporter into a food importer, culminating in the 1980s crisis and Nyerere's own 1985 admission that the policy had failed. Latin American import-substitution industrialization ran the same variant with different instruments: 100%+ effective protection rates, balance-of-payments crises, uncompetitive national champions. The differentiator against East Asia, isolated by Amsden and Wade, is *reciprocity*: subsidies conditional on export performance — the world market as external examiner — with withdrawal on failure. Algorapolis's own communitarian grounding (Ubuntu, Ujamaa) makes this case load-bearing rather than decorative: a self-reliance doctrine authored from Tanzania must encode the mechanism by which Tanzania's own implementation failed — support without expiry, no external examiner, no exit path — or it is not a doctrine but an identity. Classification: **negative specification (Variant A)** — self-reliance as measured threshold with reciprocity grading is incorporable; self-reliance as identity captured by parastatal rent-seekers is the cautionary core.

### 3.5 China's Industrial Overmatch (2001–2026) — The Adversary Ledger

China holds ~31% of global manufacturing value-added (fifteen consecutive years as the world's largest manufacturer; more than the US, Japan, and Germany combined), 53–70% of global shipbuilding, a ballistic-missile inventory up 147% since 2020 (≥3,150), PLAN battle force growth from 370 toward 435 ships by 2030, and SIPRI-measured defense spending of $336 billion (2025, the 31st consecutive annual increase), on industrial subsidies of ~1.7% of GDP (CSIS *Red Ink*). It runs its own strategic-sufficiency doctrine — dual circulation, the "new whole-nation system," food/energy/finance security planning, a ~1.4-billion-barrel oil stockpile (≈3x the US SPR) — and has field-tested chokepoint weaponization (gallium/germanium 2023, antimony 2024, the April 2025 heavy-rare-earth license regime, the November 2025 timed suspension as bargaining leverage). The brittleness ledger is equally quantified: 290% total debt/GDP, $8–12 trillion of LGFV obligations, a shrinking workforce, 18–21% youth unemployment, semiconductors as the #1 import (~$300–400 billion/year with EUV blocked), 73% oil-import dependence with roughly half transiting Hormuz, and sanctions adaptation (CIPS +43% to $24.45 trillion; RMB 2%→8.5% of FX) that erodes external leverage against it on a 5–15 year horizon. Classification: **adversary dual specification** — overmatch is a capability stock, not a guarantee, financed by the soft budget constraint (Variant A in macroeconomic form) and weaponized at chokepoints (Variant B); specified as Case Study 11.

### 3.6 The US Deindustrialization (2000–2026) — The Efficiency Trap End-to-End

The policy chain (PNTR October 2000 → WTO accession December 2001 → offshoring wave), the measured atrophy (19.6M→12.6M employment; ~91K plants; ~1/3 of decline from multinational offshoring), the chokepoint concentration (~90% adversary rare-earth refining; 13–17% direct / ~47% production-function API exposure; >60% of foundry output on one island; 232x shipyard asymmetry), and the runway discovery (ratio ≈ 0.01, found in simulation, not procurement) constitute the complete Failure Mode 1 specimen — including the political layer, where the China Shock's non-recovering local labor markets feed the polarization channel. The CHIPS-era response measures the rebuild slope: TSMC Arizona 4nm in H1 2025 (~4.5 years from announcement), US fabs at ~30% cost premium and ~2x construction time, a 67K-worker gap by 2030, and the NDIS (January 2024) codifying what the doctrine formalizes. Classification: **negative specification (Mode 1)**; specified as Case Study 10.

### 3.7 The CHIPS Act (2022–2026) — The Live Test of the Cure

Money is being spent at scale ($52.7 billion appropriations, a 25% ITC, TSMC's $165 billion committed) and capacity is being built (Taiwan-parity yields reported; Samsung slipped to 2027; Intel 10% nationalized August 2025), but the binding constraint is the know-how clock: subsidies constitute under 10% of the investment that matters (Miller), the workforce gap is 59–146K, and process-engineer formation runs 4–6 years plus 18–36 months of on-the-job training. The cure works on a decade clock — which is exactly why DP-PC-10 (multi-administration continuity as constitutional parameter) and DP-PC-6 (no target scored achieved below 80% occupational pipeline fill-rate) exist. Classification: **live test** — partial success under observed slippage; the empirical calibration for every published reindustrialization timeline (1.5–3x).

### 3.8 The Cross-Case Grammar

The seven cases compress into a quantitative grammar. **Sufficiency = (skill stock × conversion speed × redundancy), externally graded**: the skill stock is the tacit-knowledge carriers (the WWII conversion's fuel; the CHIPS-era bottleneck), conversion speed is the polity's ability to redirect output without breaking constitutional order (the 40%-of-GNP datum), redundancy is days-of-cover per strategic input, and external grading is the Amsden reciprocity standard — the world market as examiner, the single mechanism-level difference between East Asia's successes and Tanzania/ISI's failures, directly encodable as conditional law. **Capture = support without expiry + no external examiner + no exit path** — identical signature from Ujamaa parastatals to the sugar program to zombie firms. **Dominance = coercive use of monopoly, negative-NPV beyond ~5–10 years** — identical signature from the 1973 embargo to NABEP, and the reason the doctrine's mirror cap is an economic law rather than a moral aspiration. Sufficiency carries a cost ceiling (1.8% friend-shoring; stockpiles cheaper); autarkic dominance does not (China's 290% debt/GDP coexisting with overmatch is the negative exhibit).

## SECTION 4: THE DOCTRINE — STRATEGIC SUFFICIENCY WITHOUT ADVERSARIAL DOMINANCE

### 4.1 Definition

The polity maintains the capacity — domestic and allied — to produce, procure, or substitute every input whose interruption would compromise essential functions within its constitutional time-buffers, at a bounded fiscal and efficiency cost, under anti-capture circuitry, and without ever holding a coercive chokepoint position over any other polity. Every term is load-bearing: *capacity*, not output (the WWII conversion datum, not the stockpile volume); *constitutional time-buffers*, not just-in-time (30/60/90 days by register tier); *bounded cost* (the IMF 1.8–7% envelope governs escalation); *anti-capture circuitry* (the boundary problem, Section 5); and *without coercive chokepoint positions* (the mirror cap, without which sufficiency decays into dominance).

### 4.2 Component One: Criticality Machinery — What Counts as Critical

A Composite Criticality Index (CCI: supply concentration, adversary-origin exposure, substitutability-inverse, demand growth, and essentiality tier, weighted and published) recomputed on a fixed cadence, feeding a **two-register architecture**: a broad Critical Register (monitoring only) and a narrow Strategic Register (triggers obligations) — the structural guard against mission-creep, since a subsidy menu cannot grow out of a monitoring list that carries no subsidies. The world's list machinery supplies the discipline: USGS re-lists roughly three-yearly (50→60 minerals); DoD's critical-technology list *shrank* from 14 to 6 areas (November 2025), proving de-listing is procedurally possible; the WHO Essential Medicines List applies a cost-effectiveness criterion that keeps the list from becoming a rent-seeking venue. Scoring-weight changes require Agora-level approval, not agency discretion — the USGS expansion's politically-influenced additions are the cautionary case. A list that cannot shrink is a capture surface; a list that shrinks on evidence is a sensor.

### 4.3 Component Two: Thresholds — How Much Is Enough, and How Much Adversary Concentration Is Too Much

Adversary-graded concentration caps, measured on **adversary-adjusted effective control** (counting transshipment and upstream ownership, not gross imports — the API case's ~3x understatement is the calibration): ≤65% single third-country for civilian-critical inputs (the EU CRMA mirror), ≤40% for Strategic Register inputs (the DP-ESG-2 draft standard), ≤25% for war-essential inputs. Stage-differentiated domestic floors in the CRMA spirit (≥10% extraction, ≥40% processing, ≥25% recycling, scaled by register tier) — encoding the insight that *processing, not extraction, is where chokepoints bind* (China's rare-earth position is refining share, not mining share). Time-buffer floors: 30 days war-essential, 60 days civilian-critical, 90 days energy (the IEA obligation). Redundancy tiers: N+1 for the Critical Register, N+2 for the Strategic Register, 2N only for constitutional-locks-tier functions — 2N elsewhere is over-engineering, the autarky trap in engineering form.

### 4.4 Component Three: The Intervention Ladder — When Market Sourcing Is Overridden

Market sourcing is overridden ONLY when the coded conjunction holds (CCI above Strategic threshold AND adversary-adjusted share above cap AND cheaper rungs failed or time-infeasible), and then through the cheapest-first ladder: (1) diversification and multi-sourcing; (2) friend-shoring and allied co-production; (3) strategic stockpile build; (4) capacity guarantee with output-contingent incentives (the India PLI pattern); (5) state production of last resort. Each escalation publishes its expected GDP cost in the Agora docket before activation (the IMF calibration: friend-shoring ~1.8% of global GDP, reshoring ~4.5%, fragmentation up to 7%) — transparency is the price of the override, and wrong-tool selection (tariffs against scarcity, stockpiles against coercion, capacity programs against a demand problem) is a reviewable doctrine violation. This is the Modality-Matching Principle applied to sourcing: physical-scarcity risk takes stockpile rungs, coercion risk takes diversification rungs, capability-decay risk takes capacity rungs.

### 4.5 Component Four: Anti-Capture Circuitry — The Boundary Problem

The decisive component, because the world's converging statutes all have thresholds and none have circuitry. Eight guards: (1) **sunset-and-fresh-reauthorization** — every sufficiency measure auto-expires within five years; renewal requires a new CCI score; extension beyond one term requires supermajority constitutional-lock vote; (2) **adjudicated security justification** — no agency self-certifies "national security" (the WTO Article XXI lesson); adversary-status declarations are pre-registered with evidence and tested by an independent Adjudication Chamber before override instruments activate; (3) **output-contingent incentives only** — no open-ended price supports (the sugar-program failure mode), auto-termination at cost-parity; (4) **harm disclosure** — every override publishes modeled consumer and GDP cost in the Agora docket; (5) **beneficiary-concentration monitors** with automatic review triggers (the ~10,000-grower pattern); (6) **procurement fee-recapture** — no cost-plus without milestone-linked clawbacks (the F-35 incentive-fee pattern is prohibited); (7) **stockpile release locks** — reserves release only on coded objective triggers (physical interruption, IEA-coordinated emergency), never price management (the SPR 2021–22 politicization is the cautionary case); (8) **anti-monopoly-of-remedy** — two or more independent suppliers or technologies per Strategic Register item. The Amsden reciprocity standard — performance milestones, withdrawal on failure, forced exit paths — is the conditionality spine running through all eight.

### 4.6 Component Five: The No-Dominance Clause — The Mirror Half

Algorapolis entities must not supply more than 65% of any external polity's consumption of a Register-class input and must not deliberately construct chokepoint positions (the Farrell–Newman chokepoint effect, bounded). Export restrictions are defensive, time-limited, Adjudication-Chamber-reviewed, and carry sunset — no standing embargo policy. Hub-transparency reciprocity applies wherever the polity occupies hub positions. The reliable-supplier framing (the Australian template) governs external posture: sufficiency targets are insurance floors, never market-conquest goals. And the legitimacy ordering for external inputs is fixed: domestic capacity → diversified market purchases → multilateral pooled mechanisms (the IEA model) → bilateral contracts with sovereign equals — never extraction concessions layered on regime-changed hosts. The clause is not altruism; it is the empirical law of Section 1.3 constitutionalized: weaponization destroys the weapon, and a polity that refrains from holding chokepoints spends nothing maintaining positions that would self-liquidate anyway.

## SECTION 5: THE CONVERGENT STATUTORY PRECEDENT AND THE BOUNDARY PROBLEM

The doctrine is not an invention; it is a completion. The EU Economic Security Strategy (2023) established the promote/protect/partner structure with a rolling four-domain risk assessment (supply chains, critical infrastructure, technology leakage, economic coercion); the EU Critical Raw Materials Act (2024) is the world's most explicit sufficiency statute (≥10% extraction, ≥40% processing, ≥25% recycling floors; ≤65% single third-country cap; 2030 horizon); Japan's Economic Security Promotion Act (2022) contributes the four-pillar architecture including ex-ante critical-infrastructure review and the secret-patent regime; India's Production-Linked Incentive scheme (14 sectors) supplies the output-contingent incentive pattern; the US critical-minerals machinery (USGS), CISA's 16-sector designation, and the first National Defense Industrial Strategy (January 2024) codify the diagnosis. The measurement layer is mature: ECI for economic complexity, the NY Fed's GSCPI for supply-chain pressure, IEA 90-day obligations, NATO production commitments.

What every one of these systems lacks is the boundary machinery: WTO Article XXI's self-judging clause enabled steel protectionism; the US sugar program transfers $2.4–4 billion annually to roughly 10,000 growers under a security-adjacent framing that has survived every reform wave since 1934; the Jones Act locked in a high-cost maritime constituency; the F-35 program paid over $1 billion in incentive fees amid late delivery on a $2.1 trillion lifecycle; and the World Bank's synthesis documents systematic sunset-clause erosion across industrial policy. **The asymmetry — thresholds exist everywhere, anti-capture circuitry exists nowhere — is the precise specification of Algorapolis's contribution.** The framework does not need to invent sufficiency doctrine; it needs to attach the Civilizational Immune System to it. That is what DP-PC-9 does, and why the doctrine's hardest problem is not calibration but boundary maintenance.

Long-termism is not a policy the framework must import from the reindustrializers — it is native Algorapolis architecture (Volume I and II, the Ark, high-continuity assets) — which means the framework can *grant the diagnosis its full strength* while rejecting the cure's coercive turn.

The claim Algorapolis can now make, on evidence, is precise: the framework captures the entire defensible core of the reindustrialization program (criticality machinery, thresholds, buffers, multi-administration continuity, the skills substrate) and rejects the indefensible core (coercive acquisition, unaudited quantities, unexpired support, chokepoint leverage), exactly as the Family research captured the defensible core of the family-privilege critique (material floors) while rejecting custody transfer. ## NEW DESIGN PRINCIPLES — PRODUCTIVE CAPACITY AND INDUSTRIAL SOVEREIGNTY

**DP-PC-1: Critical Goods Sovereignty Baseline.** The Sovereign Logic Engine shall maintain a two-register architecture for productive capacity: a Critical Register (monitoring, telemetry, no obligations) and a Strategic Register (thresholds, buffers, and intervention triggers), both scored by a Composite Criticality Index — supply concentration, adversary-origin exposure, substitutability-inverse, demand growth, essentiality tier — recomputed on a fixed published cadence. De-listing shall be procedurally symmetrical with listing (the DoD 14→6 contraction is the precedent that lists can shrink); a cost-discipline criterion (the WHO EML pattern) bounds the Strategic Register against becoming a subsidy menu; and any change to scoring weights is an Agora-level decision, not agency discretion. An unregistered criticality list — the status quo this principle ends — is a sensor gap, not a neutral omission.

**DP-PC-2: Strategic Sufficiency Threshold.** Sufficiency thresholds are adversary-graded and stage-differentiated: single-third-country concentration caps of ≤65% (civilian-critical), ≤40% (Strategic Register), ≤25% (war-essential), measured on adversary-adjusted effective control including transshipment and upstream ownership; domestic stage floors of ≥10% extraction, ≥40% processing, ≥25% recycling scaled by register tier, encoding the processing-chokepoint law; time-buffer floors of 30 days (war-essential), 60 days (civilian-critical), 90 days (energy, IEA obligation); and redundancy tiers of N+1 (Critical), N+2 (Strategic), 2N reserved for constitutional-locks-tier functions only. The CRMA benchmarks are the statutory precedent; the German 55% gas datum is the coercibility calibration; 2N redundancy beyond the constitutional tier is classified as the autarky trap in engineering form.

**DP-PC-3: Adversarial Dependency Prohibition.** No Strategic Register input shall depend on adversary-adjusted effective control above its cap. Dependency is measured on the input's production function — transshipment content, upstream ownership, sub-tier concentration — never on gross import share, which the API case shows understates exposure by roughly 3x. Allied redundancy counts toward the cap only when the ally's own upstream for that input is not adversary-controlled. Adversary status is a pre-registered, evidence-tested, Adjudication-Chamber-reviewed declaration, not an executive label — because the modern capture path runs through adversary-designation itself.

**DP-PC-4: Sourcing Modality-Matching.** Market sourcing is overridden only when the coded conjunction holds: CCI above the Strategic threshold AND adversary-adjusted share above cap AND cheaper ladder rungs exhausted or time-infeasible. The intervention ladder is strictly cheapest-first — diversification, friend-shoring and allied co-production, strategic stockpile build, capacity guarantee with output-contingent incentives, state production of last resort — with each escalation's expected GDP cost published in the Agora docket before activation (the IMF 1.8%/4.5%/7% envelope). Modality-Matching binds: scarcity risk takes stockpile rungs, coercion risk takes diversification rungs, capability-decay risk takes capacity rungs; wrong-tool selection is a reviewable doctrine violation, because the tariff-against-scarcity error is the commonest self-inflicted wound in the historical record.

**DP-PC-5: Arsenal Continuity & Surge Capacity.** The polity shall maintain a critical-munitions runway metric (depletion days at wargame-consumption rates ÷ replenishment days) with automatic alarms below 0.1 — the CSIS finding (≈0.01) is the calibration; a civilian-convertible surge registry giving peacetime visibility of convertible capacity (the WWII conversion architecture's peacetime analog); and conversion-governance authority — pre-registered priority lists, rationing authority, public-risk-bearing finance — time-boxed with automatic expiry and demobilization provisions. Deterrence is through depth, measured as the conversion-capacity ceiling (~40% of GNP, the WWII datum), never as stockpile volume: a polity that cannot convert is not strategic no matter what it stores, and a polity that can convert is sufficient without standing autarky.

**DP-PC-6: Skills Substrate Mandate.** Skill-stock accounting — tacit-knowledge carriers as a first-class civilization metric — shall be maintained by the NDT, because the scarce reserve in every mobilization case is people, not capital (subsidies <10% of the investment that matters; money cannot compress the know-how clock). No reindustrialization target may be scored achieved while its occupational pipeline fill-rate is below 80% of forecast, and talent-pipeline lead times (4–6 years formation plus 18–36 months on-the-job) are mandatory planning variables in every capacity policy. The companion DP-EP series (ALG-SIM-DEEP-2026-012) specifies the education-side instruments this principle requires.

**DP-PC-7: Mineral & Processing Depth.** Strategic-minerals doctrine covers the full chain — reserves, processing, recycling, and substitution R&D — with processing (where chokepoints bind) as the floor priority. Chokepoint weaponization is modeled as a recurring shock class arriving as escalation ladders (controls → ban → licensed truce), not as one-off events, with the 2023–2025 gallium/germanium/antimony/rare-earth sequence — including the November 2025 timed suspension deployed as bargaining leverage — as the calibration sequence. The Apr 2025 license regime is the standing proof that the adversary treats this as a doctrine, not an emergency.

**DP-PC-8: Energy Sufficiency Anti-Dominance.** Energy policy is valid up to the sufficiency threshold and no further: any instrument whose primary function is denying another polity's access — export-first-refusals over host-nation entities, board vetoes layered on regime-changed hosts, concessions acquired under blockade or occupation — is unconstitutional, and contracts so acquired carry coercion-provenance voidability. Quantities entering sovereign decision logic require audited reserves (the unaudited 65-billion-barrel claim is the cautionary case); reserves release only on coded objective triggers, never price management; and the legitimacy ordering for external inputs is fixed: domestic capacity → diversified market purchases → multilateral pooled mechanisms → sovereign-equal bilateral contracts — never regime-change concessions. Energy dominance is the autarky/leverage trap in its purest form: negative-NPV, self-liquidating, and corrupting of the institutions that practice it.

**DP-PC-9: Protectionist Capture Guard.** Every sufficiency instrument carries the eight-point circuitry: sunset-and-fresh-reauthorization within five years with supermajority extension; adjudicated security justification through an independent chamber; output-contingent incentives only, auto-terminating at cost-parity; harm disclosure in the Agora docket; beneficiary-concentration monitors with automatic review; procurement fee-recapture (no cost-plus without milestone-linked clawbacks); stockpile release locks; and anti-monopoly-of-remedy (two or more independent suppliers per Strategic Register item). The Amsden reciprocity standard — performance milestones, withdrawal on failure, forced exit paths — is the conditionality common denominator. The empirical warrant: the sugar program, Jones Act lock-in, F-35 incentive fees, WTO Article XXI abuse, and sunset-clause erosion are the documented failure signature of sufficiency machinery without circuitry.

**DP-PC-10: Multi-Administration Continuity Lock.** Reindustrialization programs are constitutionally persisted across administrations — 10–20 year lock parameters with pre-committed escalation ladders (subsidy → conditional equity → managed divestment) — because observed programs routinely span three or more administrations, and electoral-cycle amnesia is the efficiency trap's political delivery mechanism. All published timelines carry empirically calibrated ramp-slippage factors (observed 1.5–3x on defense and semiconductor programs), and emergency organs auto-expire subject to Agora renewal. Continuity is locked, but every lock is sunsettable by design: persistence without expiry is the zombie-firm equilibrium, the Ujamaa failure, and the sugar program in constitutional form.

**DP-PC-11: Just-in-Case Reserve Architecture.** Strategic reserves are denominated in months of consumption per strategic input (food, fuel, medicine, munitions, and Strategic Register items), with multilateral sharing protocols (the IEA coordinated-release model as the non-coercive counter-template to weaponized stockpiles), transparent draw-down doctrine, and release locks per DP-PC-9. Reserves are the cheapest resilience rung (the Sheffi hierarchy: flexibility before redundancy, redundancy before capacity) and are never a price-management instrument. The adversary's own ~1.4-billion-barrel stockpile confirms the doctrine symmetrically: the reserve is the sufficiency answer, and it is the answer the adversary has already implemented.

**DP-PC-12: Blockade Graceful Degradation.** The polity shall maintain surge-substitution infrastructure — pre-approved designs, chartered capacity, statutory fast-track: institutional, not technological, speed, because Germany's floating LNG terminals in ~9 months were a permitting and chartering achievement, not an invention — and days-of-cover metrics per strategic input. Post-coercion re-entry into adversary dependency requires supermajority constitutional unlock, encoding the trust-ledger lesson that dependency re-formed under emergency is the trap's favorite re-entry path. All quantitative inputs to these mechanisms carry provenance classes (independent measurement, official claim, state media, expert estimate, viral amplification), and policy thresholds may cite classes one and four only: the sourced 232x, never the viral 350x.
