## 1. The Architecture of the System

### 1.1 The Developmental State at Full Extension

The Chinese system is a developmental state at maximum extension: the most capable instance of the genus since Meiji Japan and postwar Korea. Planning runs through five-year plans — the 14th (2021–2025) was the first with a dedicated economic-security section covering food, energy, and finance (SystemIQ) — and the food doctrine is explicit sufficiency language: "basic self-sufficiency in grain, absolute security of staple food" (SCIO, 2019), a 95% self-sufficiency target, ~700 million tonnes of annual output, 725 million by 2030. Sector programs stack on top — Made in China 2025 (2015) — and the command instrument is Xi's "new type of whole-nation system" (新型举国体制), which MERICS (Oct 2023) documents as Mao-era mobilization logic revived to close key technology gaps. Military-civil fusion fuses commercial and defense production: the merchant shipbuilding base feeds naval construction as one organism, and CSSC alone out-produces all US yards.

Xi announced "dual circulation" (双循环) in May 2020 — internal circulation as the mainstay, external subordinate — which in Algorapolis's vocabulary is a sufficiency doctrine: Beijing's own declaration that dependency on external demand and external technology had become a strategic vulnerability. The financial substrate distinguishes China from every predecessor: CSIS "Red Ink" (May 2022) estimates industrial-policy spending at ≥1.73% of GDP (~$248 billion per year in 2019) versus ~0.39% for the United States, explicitly a "conservative" floor; the IMF (Aug 2024) counts ~5,400 subsidy measures in force since 2009, roughly 95% of all GTA-tracked policies globally; and the policy banks (CDB, Exim) channel state credit at scales no Western development bank matches. Two circulating claims must be corrected explicitly. First, "$1.7 trillion in subsidies" is a garble: Red Ink's "1.7" is a *percentage of GDP*; the ~$1.7 trillion in the record is the November 2024 CNY 12 trillion local-government debt swap; cumulative Made-in-China-2025 support is separately estimated only in the low trillions. Second, "the only country with all industrial categories in the UN classification" — all 41 major, 207 medium, and 666 small categories of GB/T 4754, aligned with UN ISIC — is a PRC-official assertion (gov.cn, People's Daily, MOFCOM, Qiushi); no independent UN body publishes a completeness ranking, so it must be tagged as state framing rather than certified fact.

### 1.2 Read as Doctrine

Algorapolis reads this architecture functionally. China has built the capability half of strategic sufficiency — the patient-capital, sector-targeting, mobilization-ready machinery the DP-ESG-3 series calls developmental-state capabilities — while refusing the boundary half. Dual circulation and the reserve stockpile are legitimate sufficiency instruments in bounded form; but the same state holds processing chokepoints it has used coercively, and its subsidy machine runs without expiry, external examiner, or exit path. Amsden's export discipline is inverted into support without discipline — the mechanism by which Variant A (rent-seeking capture) accumulates *inside* a Variant B (coercive leverage) state.

## 2. The Overmatch, Quantified

### 2.1 The Verified Ratios

The overmatch is real and decisive at surge scale — which is why verified numbers matter more than viral ones. China's share of global manufacturing value-added rose from 6.4% in 2000 to ~31% in 2022 (Cambridge CIIP), and it has been the world's largest manufacturer for fifteen consecutive years (Global Times); the United States stands at ~16%, Japan ~6%, Germany ~4%. In shipbuilding, China captured 53.3% of global completions and 70% of new orders in 2024 (CSIS) and held 63–69% of 2025 orders (SCMP). The leaked ONI slide (Jul 2023) put Chinese shipyard capacity at ~23.25 million tons against under 100,000 US tons — a ~232x gap; the frequently quoted "350x" is wrong and should be corrected to ~232x, a correction that costs nothing because 232x is already decisive. The People's Liberation Army Navy fields 370-plus battle-force ships, projected at 435 by 2030 (DoD China Military Power Report 2024), against a US Navy of roughly 290–295. The missile force holds at least 3,150 ballistic missiles plus ~300 ground-launched cruise missiles (2024), with ballistic inventory up ~147% since 2020, ~500 ICBM launchers and 400 ICBMs, produced by a network of ~80 firms (Bloomberg, May 2026). SIPRI estimates 2025 defense spending at $336 billion (+7.4%, the 31st consecutive annual rise) against an official 2026 budget of RMB 1.91 trillion (~$277 billion).

### 2.2 The Weaponized Chokepoints

China accounts for ~60–70% of rare-earth mining, 85–90% of processing and refining, and more than 90% of permanent magnets (IEA; CFR); gallium ~98.7%, magnesium ~95%, tungsten ~82.7% of refining — averaging ~70% across critical minerals. This is the chokepoint stock, and 2023–2025 is the weaponization template the simulation must encode as a recurring shock class: gallium/germanium controls (Aug 2023) → graphite (Dec 2023) → antimony (2024) → MOFCOM Notice 2024 No. 46 (Dec 3, 2024), banning Ga/Ge/Sb and superhard-material exports to the US → Announcement No. 18 of 2025 (Apr 2025), the licensing regime over medium and heavy rare earths → an October 2025 expansion to five magnet rare earths → and the November 2025 one-year suspension of the ban, executed as explicit truce leverage (CSET; CSIS; NYT, Nov 2025). The USGS-modeled cost of a total gallium/germanium ban to the US is ~$3.4 billion of GDP — real, but three orders of magnitude below the collapse rhetoric. The energy and payments flanks complete the picture: China ended 2025 with ~1.4 billion barrels of crude in stocks (EIA estimate, built at ~1.1M b/d), more than three times the US SPR's 413 million barrels; CIPS cleared ¥175.49 trillion (~$24.45 trillion) in 2024, up 43%; the RMB's share of global FX transactions rose from 2% (2013) to 8.5% (mid-2025); gold rose to 7.6% of reserves; and sanctioned-oil shadow fleets find safe harbor (USCC). CFR (Mar 2026) shows RMB cross-border flows measurably weakening US sanctions leverage — the empirical basis for the 5–15-year decay horizon.

## 3. The Brittleness Ledger

The same state carries a liability ledger the overmatch discourse systematically omits, and the omission is doctrinally load-bearing. Total economy debt stands near 290% of GDP (IW Cologne); local-government financing-vehicle (LGFV) hidden debt is estimated at $8–12 trillion — roughly 50% of GDP — against ~30% official local debt (MacroPolo; RBA), with land sales having supplied 38% of local revenue as recently as 2019; the November 2024 "rescue" was a CNY 12 trillion (~$1.7 trillion) debt swap — extend-and-pretend, not deleveraging. Demography compounds the fiscal problem: population declining every year since 2022, working-age share down from 61.3% (2023) to 60.9% (2024), and urban youth unemployment between ~18% and 21% — a 21.3% peak in June 2023, 18.8% in August 2024, ~17.9% in July 2026. These constrain the shipyard welders and munitions machinists the overmatch depends on.

The technology and energy positions mirror the chokepoints China holds over others. Semiconductors are China's largest import, ahead of crude oil, at ~$300–400 billion per year (Bruegel; Heritage), while EUV lithography remains export-controlled and China approaches 5–7nm only through DUV multi-patterning (AEI) — a chokepoint *with a decay horizon*, not a permanent moat, though a live dependency today. Energy repeats the structure: 73–74% oil-import dependence, record ~11.6M b/d imports in 2025, roughly half of crude from the Arabian Gulf (the classic "80% via Malacca" is an older estimate, at best a ~70–80% model-dependent range), and ~90% of crude still seaborne despite pipeline hedges. Deflationary overcapacity is the macro symptom: 1.73%-plus-of-GDP industrial policy plus LGFV credit has plausibly passed into negative marginal returns. The doctrinal point follows: overmatch is a *capability stock, not a guarantee*, financed by the soft budget constraint — support without expiry, external examiner, or exit path. That is Mode 2 Variant A in macroeconomic form, layered inside the Variant B state. Sufficiency has a built-in cost ceiling; autarkic dominance does not, and China is now paying the unbounded term of that contract.

## 4. The NABEP Mirror: Energy Dominance as Failure Mode 2

### 4.1 The Sequence and the Terms

The US–Venezuela arrangement of August 2026 is the same failure mode fielded from the opposite, deficit-side starting point, and its provenance sequence is part of the terms. Chevron's Venezuela license was revoked in March 2025; a US blockade cut Venezuela's December 2025 exports; on January 3, 2026 a US military raid captured President Nicolás Maduro, installing Delcy Rodríguez as interim president (sanctions lifted Apr 1, 2026). The deal was announced August 28–29 ("the biggest oil deal in world history") and the White House published its fact sheet late on August 31: "…Historic Oil Agreement to Secure American Energy Dominance and Drive Venezuela's Economic Recovery." The doctrine is named in the title; the terms, corroborated across CNBC, WSJ, BBC, Reuters, and AP, are: 100-year NABEP concessions over 17 oil fields (plus 8 greenfield blocks, 1.5M b/d initial target, per Reuters); claimed proven reserves of ~65 billion barrels — a *deal-party claim with no third-party audit* (roughly one-fifth of Venezuela's ~303 billion); a 35% passive equity stake at zero cost held by the Pentagon (Office of Strategic Capital); the right to purchase 20% of output at production cost; right of first refusal on the remaining 80%; veto over board appointments with a US-citizen board majority; up to $100 billion in promised investment — *unfunded*, no syndicate identified (Yahoo Finance: "the biggest unanswered question"); and ~$200 billion in royalties and taxes over 25 years (Rodríguez: ~$209 billion at $65/bbl) — price-dependent projections. The counterparty, North American Blue Energy Partners, is a Barbados-registered private firm, Venezuela's second-largest private producer, headed by Alejandro Betancourt López, co-founded with Harry Sargeant in April 2024; five of fourteen displaced contracts were previously Chinese-operated. Legality is contested — NPR (Sep 3, 2026) quotes critics calling the deals unconstitutional "gunboat diplomacy after removing Maduro," Reuters reports lawyers demanding contract transparency — and the contract text remains unpublished.

### 4.2 The Leverage Is Overstated — and the Boomerang Is Already Scheduled

The strategic rationale — controlling "the oil China needs" — does not survive scrutiny. Less than 40% of China's crude transits Hormuz (AFP fact-check; Columbia SIPA: 45–50%); China entered 2026 holding ~1.4 billion barrels, roughly 70% of its annual Hormuz import volume (Reuters graphics); its Venezuela exposure — ~$19 billion outstanding of $105.59 billion committed across 106 loans (AidData) — is small against 11.6M b/d of diversified imports (Russia 17.9%, Saudi ~14%, Iraq ~11.3%, Malaysia transshipments ~10%). The boomerang record is consistent across every precedent: the 1973 embargo (price quadrupled) produced the IEA (1974) and strategic reserves; Russia's gas weaponization collapsed its EU share from 45% to 13% and volumes from 179 to 31 Bcm; and China's 2023–25 controls produced the Pentagon's MP Materials deal ($400 million for a 15% stake, a $110/kg NdPr price floor, a 10X magnet plant by 2028), the EU Critical Raw Materials Act (≥10% extraction, ≥40% processing, ≥25% recycling; ≤65% from any single third country by 2030) — and China's own November 2025 suspension. Every weaponized chokepoint self-destructs on a one-to-ten-year clock; dominance plays carry negative net present value beyond roughly five to ten years. The NABEP arrangement additionally inverts the legitimate crisis instrument: announced mid-war, with Hormuz flows at 4.9M b/d in Q2 2026 and Brent up 55–60%, it answered the same disruption the IEA answered on March 11, 2026 with the largest-ever coordinated stock release — where the IEA chose pooling, Washington chose acquisition.

## 5. Analysis Through Algorapolis Layers

### Layer 4 (Economics): Overmatch Economics and the Soft Budget Constraint

The overmatch is the output of a soft-budget-constraint economy: RMB 330 billion of entry subsidies and RMB 159 billion of production subsidies (BigDataChina), ~5,400 measures in force, and an LGFV complex applying the same no-exit logic. China's own answer to energy vulnerability was not autarky (impossible at 73% import dependence) but the 1.4-billion-barrel stockpile, supplier diversification, and drawdown doctrine — tapped at ~1M b/d during the 2026 Gulf shock: for fungible commodities, stockpiles beat autarky and days-of-cover beats import elimination. Sufficiency floors are affordable (friend-shoring ~1.8% of GDP versus ~4.5% for reshoring); dominance positions are expensive and self-liquidating.

### Layer 6 (Security): Full-Spectrum Adversary Doctrine

The adversary runs a full-spectrum doctrine, not a sector doctrine: dual circulation, whole-nation-system R&D, economic-security planning, reserve stockpiles, and payments infrastructure are one portfolio. Sufficiency metrics in the National Digital Twin must therefore be portfolio-level — minerals → processing → components → systems → fuels → food → payments — never single-metric. Weaponized interdependence arrives as escalation ladders (controls → ban → licensed truce), not one-off shocks, so the simulation must model weaponization events as a recurring class with empirical duration and cost parameters. Military-civil fusion — a commercial shipbuilding base ~232x larger feeding naval construction — means a defensive civilization needs a civilian-convertible surge registry, not arsenal counts; war-games must use verified ratios, never viral ones.

### Layer 3 (Governance): The Provenance Firewall

The claim that China turns out "more cruise missiles in a week than the US in a year" is the cleanest metric-corruption case on record, and its chain of mutation specifies the Sovereign Logic Engine's provenance classes: CCTV footage of an automated line (Oct 2024) → David Goldman's "1,000 cruise missile *motors* a day" (Law Liberty, May 2024) → Visegrád 24's "1,000 missiles per day" (Oct 22, 2024) → the politician's "a week beats a US year." Verified data refute the arithmetic — the Pentagon counts ~300 ground-launched cruise missiles and growth rates implying hundreds to ~1,000-plus missiles per year, while the US itself produces ~1,000–2,000 long-range missiles per year — even as the *directional* surge asymmetry stands. Every quantitative input entering the SLE therefore requires a provenance class (independent measurement / official claim / adversary state media / expert estimate / viral amplification), with policy thresholds citing only the first and fourth; the same firewall tags the unaudited 65-billion-barrel figure and the 41/207/666 claim.

### Layer 9 (Civilization): One Trap, Two Entry Points

Two polities converging on the same dominance trap from opposite starting points — the surplus-side overmatch state and the deficit-side reindustrializing state — is civilizational evidence that the trap is structural rather than cultural: the failure mode lives in the objective function (dominance), not the ideology that carries it. China entered through sufficiency-seeking that overshot into leverage; the United States entered through dependency anxiety that curdled into coercion. Both face the same exit, and only one is stable: sufficiency floors under anti-capture circuitry, with a mirror cap.

## 6. Constitutional Compatibility Assessment

### The Chinese Developmental-State Model Against the Four Guardrails

**Guardrail 1 (No Omniscience): violated in principle.** Whole-nation-system planning and five-year sector targeting presume a completeness of economic knowledge — which sectors will win, which technologies will close — that no planner holds; the resulting overcapacity is not an execution error but the predictable output of the omniscient presumption. **Guardrail 2 (No Total Behavioral Prediction):** not the binding violation in the industrial domain. **Guardrail 3 (No Centralized Social Engineering): violated structurally** — the whole-nation system is social engineering of the economy's composition itself, extended by the 14th FYP into food, energy, and finance. **Guardrail 4 (No Unrestricted Computational Control):** not binding here. The decisive failure is against the no-dominance clause: China supplies more than 65% of external polities' consumption of several register-class inputs — permanent magnets, gallium, refined rare earths — a standing mirror-cap violation, and it has *weaponized* those positions. Its sufficiency floors would be compatible; its chokepoint positions and its subsidy machine without expiry, examiner, or exit path are not.

### The NABEP Arrangement Against the Four Guardrails

**Guardrail 1: violated in the auditing sense** — an unaudited 65-billion-barrel figure enters sovereign decision logic as if measured, precisely what the audited-reserves rule forbids. **Guardrails 2 and 4:** not binding. **Guardrail 3: strained** insofar as a wartime organ redirects a whole economy's energy posture by decree. The binding failures are constitutional-exonomic: instruments whose *primary function* is denying another polity's access — right of first refusal over 80% of exports, a board veto over a host-nation entity, a 100-year term — are chokepoint positions, unconstitutional under the mirror cap; and the acquisition sequence (license revocation → blockade → raid → concession) is tainted provenance, which the SLE must flag as defaulting to voidability. The anti-capture circuitry is likewise absent: a zero-cost stake, an unpublished contract, an unfunded $100 billion promise, and a single-family counterparty. The matching-principle failure is modality: market-logic instruments (equity, concessions, ROFR) applied to a coercion-logic end, where the legitimate instruments were IEA-style pooling or sovereign-equal bilateral contracts.

### The Mirror-Cap Logic

The constitutional rule this pair fixes is asymmetrical on purpose: **a polity may hold sufficiency floors, never chokepoint positions.** Floors are cheap, self-limiting, and decay gracefully; positions are expensive, self-eroding, and decay catastrophically. China's 85–90% processing share and the NABEP's attempted 80%-of-output control are the same unconstitutional act from opposite directions — one established, one aspirational, both scheduled for the same boomerang.

## 7. Design-Principle Implications

**DP-PC-7 (Mineral & Processing Depth).** China's 2023–25 arc is the calibration set for chokepoint weaponization as a recurring shock class with escalation ladders — controls → ban → licensed truce — and for its limits: China kept ~85–90% processing but was forced into its own suspension within a year of the December 2024 ban. Mineral doctrine must cover reserves *and* processing *and* recycling *and* substitution R&D, with subsidies that ratchet down automatically as world shares diversify.

**DP-PC-8 (Energy Sufficiency Anti-Dominance).** NABEP is the boundary case made law: any instrument whose primary function is denying another polity's access — ROFR over exports, board vetoes over host-nation entities — is unconstitutional; quantities entering sovereign decision logic require third-party audit provenance (the unaudited 65 billion barrels); reserves release only on coded triggers; and the legitimacy ordering runs domestic capacity → diversified market purchases → multilateral pooling (the IEA's March 2026 release) → sovereign-equal bilateral contracts — never regime-change concessions.

**DP-PC-11 (Just-in-Case Reserve Architecture).** China's ~1.4-billion-barrel stockpile is the adversary's own proof that stockpiles beat autarky for fungible commodities: reserves denominated in months of consumption, with transparent defensive drawdown doctrine, are the cheapest resilience rung. RAND's warning that a reserve can become a geoeconomic weapon specifies the transparency requirement; the March 2026 coordinated release shows multilateral sharing working under the exact shock the NABEP play exploited.

**DP-PC-12 (Blockade Graceful Degradation).** Days-of-cover metrics per strategic input and pre-approved surge-substitution capacity follow from China's Hormuz arithmetic (~half of crude; ~3–4 months of import cover). The provenance firewall is this case's direct product: tagged quantitative inputs only, with policy thresholds citing independent measurements and expert estimates — never state-media figures or their viral descendants. Post-coercion re-entry into dependency requires supermajority unlock, because coerced dependency (Venezuela's ~$19 billion of oil-backed loans in distress) decays no less reliably than chosen dependency.

**DP-ESG-3 (Developmental-State Capabilities with Anti-Capture Circuitry + Amsden Reciprocity).** China proves the capabilities work — a 6.4%→31% manufacturing share in two decades — and proves the circuitry is mandatory: 290% debt/GDP, $8–12 trillion of hidden LGFV debt, and deflationary overcapacity are what developmental-state capability costs without expiry, external examination, and exit paths. Amsden reciprocity (export and performance milestones, withdrawal on failure, forced exit) is the missing discipline.

**DP-ESG-7 (Geopolitical Positioning with the No-Dominance Mirror Cap).** The mirror cap — never supply more than 65% of any external polity's consumption of a register-class input — is the constitutional expression of the boomerang law. China's standing violation (magnets, gallium, refined rare earths) and the US attempt to manufacture a symmetric violation (Venezuelan oil under ROFR) are the same unconstitutional act; the cap binds the self, never the adversary, because chokepoints are self-liquidating assets. Geopolitical positioning is legitimate as resilience, redundancy, and diversification-of-partners — never as monopoly.

## Closing Verdict

The dual verdict is symmetrical. **China demonstrates that Failure Mode 2, Variant B is fielded, decaying, and internally costly**: the overmatch is real at every verified ratio, but it is a capability stock financed by the soft budget constraint, carried on a 290%-of-GDP debt ledger with a shrinking workforce and a blocked lithography frontier, and its coercive instruments have already triggered the counter-mobilizations (MP Materials, EU CRMA, its own suspension) that erode them on a one-to-ten-year clock. **The NABEP arrangement demonstrates that the reindustrialization cure can replicate the same trap**: a polity alarmed by dependency answered with blockade, regime change, and a 100-year chokepoint acquisition whose reserves are unaudited, whose funding does not exist, whose legality is contested, and whose target's adaptation capacity already blunted the leverage it was built to hold. Sufficiency without dominance is not a moral preference between these two failures; it is the only equilibrium that survives its own operation — floors are cheap and self-limiting, positions expensive and self-liquidating — and a durable civilization's constitution must permit the first and forbid the second.
