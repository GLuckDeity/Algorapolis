## 1. The Values-Transmission Problem

### 1.1 Education Is Inherently a Values Enterprise

The Substrate-Level Education research (Part IV) established education as a civilizational substrate requiring equity-quality parity, cognitive justice, and epistemic non-subordination (EDU-1 through EDU-19). What that research did not confront is that every curriculum decision above the purely technical embeds answers to contested questions — what history deserves telling, which literatures matter, how conflict is narrated, what a good citizen is. The Abidjan-dreaming education reformer and the critical-pedagogy theorist agree on this much: schools are not values-neutral pipes delivering literacy. The governance question is therefore never *whether* values are transmitted but *who* controls the transmission: the state, the family, the community, the profession, or the platform. The answer embodied in most existing systems — state curriculum monopoly with family exit rights priced near zero — is precisely the configuration that both political traditions intermittently attempt to weaponize, each in its own direction.

### 1.2 The Convergent Jurisprudence

The world's legal systems, independently, have converged on the same partition. *Pierce v. Society of Sisters* (1925) — decided against Oregon's compulsory public-schooling law aimed at Catholic immigrant families — holds that "the child is not the mere creature of the State" and that parents may direct the child's education; the state's legitimate power is to *regulate quality*, not to monopolize provision. *Wisconsin v. Yoder* (1972) adds the values dimension: compulsory schooling that would override the community's values transmission (Amish) fails absent harm. *Meyer v. Nebraska* (1923) — a teacher convicted for teaching German to a child, under a WWI nativist law — is the founding statement that the state cannot use curriculum to engineer children's cultural content. The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, Article 18, assigns parents the primary responsibility for the child's development; Article 29 frames education's values aims as respecting the child's own cultural identity, language, and values *while* inculcating human-rights respect — the double lock: family-cultural content plus universal rights floor. The European framework (Protocol 1, Article 2 of the ECHR) states the exact architecture: "the State shall respect the right of parents to ensure such education and teaching in conformity with their own religious and philosophical convictions" while operating state education. The convergence is the same rule Algorapolis states as the family severability principle: the state guarantees the floor (literacy, rights, numeracy, science); families and communities direct the creed; and no institution — state, church, corporation, or movement — holds both levers at once.

### 1.3 Why This Is Now Urgent

The urgency is not hypothetical. Curriculum capture has become a primary terrain of global ideological competition: the Carnegie Endowment (2025) documents family and education policy as explicit vectors in international information warfare; multiple governments have rewritten history curricula as instruments of regime narrative (Turkey's textbook Islamization program, Hungary's post-2010 curriculum nationalization, India's NCERT revision controversies, Poland's 2019–2023 reforms and partial reversal); and in the United States, the 2021–2025 period produced simultaneous capture attempts from opposed directions — speech-content legislation in several states and values-curriculum mandates in others, with public schooling becoming a declining-trust institution between them. The pattern is symmetric across regimes, which is the Algorapolis analytic requirement: the defense must bind all capture directions, not the currently fashionable one.

## 2. The Mechanics of Curriculum Capture

### 2.1 The Seven-Stage Pattern

Study 18's ideological-capture model (capture proceeds through staffing, then standards, then curriculum, then assessment, then enforcement, then purging of alternatives, then constitutional entrenchment) applies to education with unusual precision because education systems concentrate the exact assets capture requires: a standardized content layer, a captive audience, an assessment apparatus that certifies truth, and a monopoly position. Stage one is never announced as ideology: it arrives as a quality reform, a civics initiative, an anti-bias program, or a heritage restoration. Each is individually defensible; the capture signature appears only in the direction of asymmetry — the reform constrains one side's values content and never the reformers' own.

### 2.2 The Historical Negative Specifications

The completed historical cases share the full seven-stage pattern. The Soviet pedagogical state made curriculum the primary transmission instrument of the regime's worldview, with Pavlik Morozov hagiography as its terminal specification — the child cultivated as informant against family values, the exact ideological-custody-transfer vector (Vector 4) that the Family and Social Fabric research locks against. Indigenous residential and boarding schools (US/Canada, 1869–1960s) used compulsory schooling as the assimilation engine, explicitly designed to overwrite family and cultural values transmission, with intergenerational trauma measurable into the third and fourth generation. These are not remote analogies: they are the realized endpoints of the two capture directions — the state's creed replacing the family's, left-coded and right-coded respectively — and they share every architectural feature: monopoly provision, compulsory attendance, exit priced out, assessment as doctrinal certification, and the child positioned as the object of ideological formation.

### 2.3 The Contemporary Substrate: Platforms and AI Tutors

The capture surface has migrated. Curricular content for children is now substantially delivered by algorithmic systems — video platforms, adaptive software, AI tutors — whose content-selection logic is optimizing engagement, not any democratically set standard, and whose provenance is opaque. Part IV of this research program documented the learning-science layer (AI tutoring's procedural gains, its conceptual-understanding deficits); the values-transmission layer is worse: a recommendation system is a curriculum (it sequences content, models attention, and normalizes worldviews) that no education ministry authored, no family chose, and no audit inspects. The Chinese textbook-centralization model and the Western platform model are, structurally, the same subsystem — values transmission without provenance — differing only in whether the values-author is a ministry or an engagement optimizer. The provenance and transparency architecture (DP-EV-6) is specified against both simultaneously.

## 3. Education Pluralism: The Institutional Third Way

### 3.1 The Dutch and German Settlements

The institutional alternative to monopoly-versus-exit is education pluralism: state-funded, quality-regulated, plural provision. The Netherlands' pillarization settlement (1917) funded schools of all worldviews on equal footing — a constitutional architecture born of a school war, and the standing proof that the state can guarantee education without owning its content. Germany's post-1949 dual system (state schools plus state-funded confessional and alternative schools, with a private-school licensing regime that regulates quality and ideological independence from the state) was explicitly designed against the capture lessons of both the Nazi curriculum state and the East German one. Berner's comparative program and Garnett's legal theory (2020) generalize the pattern: plural systems achieve comparable or better equity outcomes than monopoly systems while eliminating the zero-sum values war over a single curricular throne, because no single capture event controls the whole system's content.

### 3.2 The Design Pattern

Pluralism's architecture, stated in Algorapolis terms: **funding follows the child, not the provider**; providers are plural (public, community, religious, independent) under common outcome floors; entry and exit are real rights (portable funding, no exit taxation, capacity guarantees); and the state's assessment power is confined to the floor (literacy, numeracy, science, rights) and structurally barred from the creed layer. This is the same severability move as the family's material floor (DP-FS-2): guarantee the conditions, never own the content. Pluralism fails when any of its four components fails — when funding is provider-locked (monopoly by subsidy), when exit is priced (capture by attrition), when assessment creeps into values content (capture by examination), or when one provider's failure is generalized into system control (capture by crisis). The DP-EV series locks each of these failure points.

### 3.3 What Pluralism Is Not

Pluralism is not deregulation. The German system's licensing regime and the Dutch system's inspection regime maintain quality floors; Garnett's "progressive pluralism" argument is precisely that pluralism requires a strong regulatory state to prevent insularity (the isolation failure mode — schools that exit the rights floor itself). The Algorapolis position must therefore be specified against both failure directions: state monopoly (capture) and unregulated exit (insularity). The floor-barriers structure — mandatory rights education, science floors, abuse screening through the formalized family intervention threshold, and inter-school assessment transparency — holds the middle.

## 4. Homeschooling: The Exit Right and Its Boundaries

### 4.1 The Legal Spectrum

Homeschooling law is the live edge of the values-transmission question because it is exit in its purest form. The German constitutional prohibition (1918 Prussian schooling tradition, 1949 Basic Law reading education as a state function, upheld by the Federal Constitutional Court and the ECtHR in *Konrad v. Germany*, 2006) sits at one pole: the state may enforce school attendance to guarantee socialization and integration. The United States sits at the other: a legal right in all fifty states with regulation varying from substantive (portfolio review, testing) to nominal, on the strength of the *Pierce/Yoder* line. Between them: France's declaration regime, Sweden's near-prohibition after the 2010 clampdown, Finland's permissive registration, and the United Kingdom's light-touch regime currently under review following abuse-case pressure.

### 4.2 The Evidence and Its Limits

The academic outcomes literature is contested and selection-biased (homeschooling families are non-randomly structured), with structured homeschooling showing adequate-to-strong academic outcomes and unstructured variants showing deficits; the socialization literature shows no uniform deficit. The evidence that matters architecturally is different: the abuse concealed by homeschooling's opacity in the UK review cases, and the German rationale — that a common schooling experience is the integration guarantee of a fragmented society. Algorapolis's architecture resolves the tension with the same instruments as the family domain: homeschooling is a real exit right (DP-FS-9, pluralism) bounded by the outcome floor (literacy, science, rights education), the formalized intervention threshold (abuse screening through the same auditable evidence classes as any family — no homeschooling-specific suspicion regime, which would be threshold creep), and registration (data that serves the child's floor rights, held under the family privacy boundary, not a family-surveillance instrument).

### 4.3 The Specification Logic

The specification logic is symmetric distrust: the state must not use education-compulsion as a values instrument (the German risk, *Meyer/Pierce*'s core holding), and the family must not use exit rights as an isolation instrument (the insularity risk, Garnett's floor argument). Both are realized risks with named historical cases. The DP-EV-4 regulation floor holds both sides: registration with outcome floors and the formalized threshold; neither prohibition nor unregulated exit.

## 5. The Neutrality Architecture

### 5.1 The Dual-Layer Curriculum, Extended

Part IV established the Dual-Layer Curriculum Mandate (EDU-11): a common core (literacy, numeracy, science, civic rights) and a plural cultural layer (community-controlled content within the rights floor). The values-transmission research extends it with the firewall specification: the two layers are not just sequential but **institutionally separated** — floor assessment is machine-checkable and uniform; cultural-layer content is family-community governed and never assessed by the state. The boundary is policed in both directions: values content cannot leak down into floor assessment (the capture direction — assessing the creed), and floor content cannot be locally vetoed (the insularity direction — refusing the science). The Contested Formalization Protocol (three-team rule) governs boundary disputes, because boundary classification is precisely the ambiguity that capture attempts exploit.

### 5.2 The Curriculum Capture Monitor

The NDT's education layer gains a capture-telemetry specification on the pattern of the Family Integrity Index: text-drift analysis on curricular materials across the seven-stage signature (which content changes, in which direction, at what rate, with what asymmetry); provenance verification of all materials (authorship, funding, licensing); provider-concentration metrics (market-share of content sources); and exit-rate telemetry (family exit from providers — the demand-side signal that something has shifted). Capture telemetry is threshold-gated to automatic constitutional review, integrating with the Civilizational Immune System's Ideological Entropy Index rather than creating a parallel instrument. The design principle is the Study 18 method applied to the education stack: capture is detected as asymmetry and trajectory, never as the content of any particular view.

### 5.3 The Teacher Layer

Teachers in compulsory schooling occupy a fiduciary position toward other people's children, which the architecture specifies as content-neutral facilitation within the dual-layer curriculum: advocacy for political or religious positions in the compulsory floor layer is an employment-standard violation, while the cultural layer (a religious school's religion class, a community school's tradition courses) is governed by its own community within the rights floor. The specification deliberately does not extend to higher education or to teachers' off-duty citizenship — the neutrality obligation is scoped to the captive-audience, state-funded, compulsory layer where the fiduciary logic applies. Boundary disputes route through the same Contested Formalization machinery.

### 5.4 Provenance for the Algorithmic Layer

The platform/AI-tutor layer inherits the C2PA-style provenance mandate from the Information Warfare research (IW-1 through IW-12): educational content reaching children through algorithmic delivery carries verifiable provenance (author, funder, model, content-modification chain), engagement-optimization is disallowed as a content-selection objective in compulsory-education contexts (selection must follow declared pedagogical standards), and AI tutors are bound by the same floor/cultural-layer separation as human teachers — the tutor teaches the floor, never the creed. This closes the platform-capture vector identified in §2.3: a curriculum nobody authored is a curriculum the architecture does not accept.

## 6. Design Principles — Values Transmission and Curriculum Neutrality

**Principle 1 — Curriculum Firewall Principle (DP-EV-1):** The SLE shall enforce a strict institutional separation between the outcome floor and the values layer of education. The floor — literacy, numeracy, science, human-rights education, civic procedure — is common, machine-assessable, and uniform across all providers. The cultural and values layer is governed by families and communities within the rights floor and shall never be assessed, scored, ranked, or standardized by the state. Values content may not be introduced into floor assessment, and floor content may not be locally vetoed. The Contested Formalization Protocol governs boundary classification disputes. This extends EDU-11 (Dual-Layer Curriculum Mandate) with the firewall specification and encodes *Pierce*, *Yoder*, and ECHR Protocol 1 Article 2.

**Principle 2 — Curriculum Capture Monitor Principle (DP-EV-2):** The NDT shall maintain capture telemetry over the education content system, integrated with the Civilizational Immune System: text-drift and asymmetry analysis on curricular materials across the seven-stage capture signature; provenance verification of authorship and funding; provider-concentration metrics; and family exit-rate telemetry. Capture is detected as asymmetry and trajectory, never as the content of any particular view. Threshold breach triggers automatic constitutional review with delayed modification protocols. The residential-school and Soviet-pedagogy cases are the negative specifications; the 2021–2025 multi-directional capture attempts are the live calibration set.

**Principle 3 — Educational Provision Pluralism Principle (DP-EV-3):** Education funding shall follow the child across a plural provider system — public, community, religious, and independent — under common outcome floors, with real entry and exit rights: portable per-child funding, no exit taxation, and capacity guarantees that prevent exit rights from being priced out by scarcity. No provider may hold monopoly position in any jurisdiction, and no provider's failure may be generalized into system-level content control. This specifies DP-FS-9's institutional mechanism on the Dutch pillarization and German dual-system pattern.

**Principle 4 — Homeschooling Regulation Floor Principle (DP-EV-4):** Homeschooling is a protected exit right, subject to a regulation floor: registration, outcome-floor verification (literacy, science, rights education), and abuse screening through the formalized family intervention threshold (DP-FS-3) — with identical evidence classes and proof standards as for schooled children. Neither prohibition (the German model's integration rationale may not be used to eliminate the exit right) nor unregulated exit (the insularity failure mode) is permitted. Homeschooling-specific suspicion regimes are prohibited as threshold creep; family privacy boundaries (DP-FS-8) apply to all data collected under the floor.

**Principle 5 — Educator Neutrality Scope Principle (DP-EV-5):** Educators in the compulsory, state-funded floor layer hold a fiduciary, content-neutral role: advocacy for political or religious positions within floor instruction is an employment-standard violation, enforceable through ordinary professional and employment law with audit support. The obligation is strictly scoped to the compulsory captive-audience layer and does not extend to higher education, adult education, or educators' off-duty citizenship. The cultural layer's instruction is governed by its own community within the rights floor. The scope boundary itself is a protected classification, contestable only through the Contested Formalization Protocol.

**Principle 6 — Educational Materials Provenance Principle (DP-EV-6):** All educational content reaching children — textbooks, software, platform-delivered media, and AI-tutor outputs — shall carry verifiable provenance: authorship, funding source, content-modification chain, and, for algorithmic systems, the content-selection objective. Engagement optimization is prohibited as a content-selection objective in compulsory-education contexts; selection must follow declared, auditable pedagogical standards. AI tutors operating in the floor layer are bound by the floor/cultural-layer separation. This closes the platform-capture vector and extends the IW provenance architecture (IW-1 through IW-12) to the education stack.
